This project is a decentralized, blockchain-powered election system designed to be impossible to rig and easy for anyone to use. Traditional voting systems rely on trust. This platform replaces trust with Mathematics. By using the Ethereum blockchain, every vote is a permanent, verifiable piece of history that cannot be altered, deleted, or forged.
